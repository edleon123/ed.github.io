Free Energy Landscapes of a Membrane Transporter

June 15, 2026 · Tags: metadynamics, free energy, transporters

Motivation

Understanding how membrane transporters alternate between inward- and outward-facing states is key to drug design. We used well-tempered metadynamics to reconstruct the free energy surface along collective variables describing the conformational change.

Simulation Setup

The system was built with CHARMM-GUI, embedded in a POPC bilayer, and solvated with TIP3P water. All simulations ran with GROMACS 2023 patched with PLUMED. 


# Example PLUMED input
cv1: DISTANCE ATOMS=345,678
cv2: TORSION ATOMS=123,456,789,1011
metad: METAD ARG=cv1,cv2 SIGMA=0.1,0.2 HEIGHT=1.2 PACE=500 ...

Results

The free energy surface revealed two major minima separated by a ~5 kcal/mol barrier, consistent with experimental turnover rates. We identified a key salt bridge that stabilizes the outward-facing state.

Takeaway

Metadynamics with carefully chosen CVs can map complex conformational landscapes in membrane proteins. Next step: umbrella sampling validation.
