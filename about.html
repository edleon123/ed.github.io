About Me

I’m a PhD student in the Premila Research Group at Saint Louis University. My work focuses on biological systems using computational chemistry. I am currently investigating crowded models to mimic all-atom cytoplasm to look at accurate simulations.

Methods: Molecular dynamics (NAMD, AMBER), enhanced sampling (RAMD, SMD), QM/MM, and force field development.

Systems: Glycolytic enzymes, crowded environments, and drug–target complexes.
